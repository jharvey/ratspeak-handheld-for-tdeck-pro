HELP: WAYPOINTS AND PLACES

All on the map (L).

Mark a waypoint: move the map so the spot is in the centre (W A S D), press P, type a name (or Enter keeps WP1, WP2 ...), Enter.

Go to one: N steps through them. A dashed line runs from you to it: "W2 BALLFIELD 2.1km SW". Del twice removes the selected one. List of all: M, S, 1, 9.

Find a place (town, lake, summit):
1. F, type the start of the name. Add a state to narrow it: "conway nh".
2. Enter shows the 6 nearest matches.
3. A digit jumps there. P right after saves it as a waypoint. ^ closes the list.

Track (breadcrumbs): M, S, 1, 0 (row 10) turns it on; a dotted line shows where you walked. To clear it: M, S, 1, V down to row 11 (Clear track), Enter twice.
