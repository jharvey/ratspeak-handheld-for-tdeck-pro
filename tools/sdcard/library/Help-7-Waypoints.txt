HELP: WAYPOINTS AND PLACES

All on the map (A).

Mark a waypoint: move the map so the spot is in the centre (T F V H), press Alt+P, type a name (or Enter keeps WP1, WP2 ...), Enter.

Go to one: P steps through them. A dashed line runs from you to it: "W2 BALLFIELD 2.1km SW". Alt+SYM (Alt and SYM together) twice removes the selected one. List of all: M, S, 1, 9.

Find a place (town, lake, summit):
1. L, type the start of the name. Add a state to narrow it: "conway nh".
2. Enter shows the 6 nearest matches.
3. A digit jumps there. Alt+P right after saves it as a waypoint. ^ closes the list.

Track (breadcrumbs): M, S, 1, 0 (row 10) opens the Track page:
1 Record track on/off: a point every 20 m moved, or every 10 min standing; a dotted line on the map.
2 Track back: a line to the earlier track points, one after the other, back to the start (on the map: B).
3 Export as GPX: ratdeckpro/tracks/track-<date>-<time>.gpx, for Google Earth / Gaia / OsmAnd.
4 Clear track: Enter twice.
The track logs when the GPS is on anyway (Full, Balanced every 15 min ...). Custom power mode has "Track wakes GPS" for more points.
Units (m/km or ft/mi): M, S, 1, row 11.
