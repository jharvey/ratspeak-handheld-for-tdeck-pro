WALKIE-TALKIE

Talk live with another RatSpeak unit, over WiFi.
- Both on the same WiFi (Settings S.3): the call uses it.
- No WiFi: your unit makes its own hotspot for the call and the invite goes over LoRa.
  The other unit joins the hotspot by itself when you answer, so the two units must be
  close (WiFi range, roughly 50-100 m in the open). After the call both go back to their
  usual WiFi.

CALL
1. Contacts (C) or Inbox (I): pick the person, Enter for their card.
2. Press W. The unit sends a call invite (an encrypted message) and shows "calling...".

ANSWER
- The other unit shows "<name> is calling you": Enter = answer, ^ = decline.
- An unanswered call ends after 60 s.

TALK
- Space = talk. The screen says TALKING. Space again = stop and listen.
- Only one side talks at a time (like a radio): the screen shows LISTENING, or
  THEY TALK while the other side speaks.
- ^ = hang up. The other side sees "the other side hung up".

GOOD TO KNOW
- Speech is Codec2 at 3200 bit/s, encrypted with a key made for each call.
- If nothing is heard for 10 s (WiFi lost), the call ends.
- The chat shows "[walkie-talkie call]" for each invite.
- "Too far for WiFi: come closer" = the other unit could not reach the hotspot in 20 s.
- In Typical mode the walkie-talkie can be turned off (S.1.5).
