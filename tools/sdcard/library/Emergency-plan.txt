USING THIS RADIO (short)
Switch on: hold the top side button 2 seconds.
Map: A (or M, then A). Arrow home: M, S, 2, row 13.
Message: C (contacts), pick a name, type, Enter. Quick answers: MIC, then Q.
SOS: hold the top side button 3 seconds (sends your position to your SOS contacts).
Switch off when done: M, S, 5, row 13. Off, the battery lasts months; on, a day or two.
