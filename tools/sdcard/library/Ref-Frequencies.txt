QUICK FREQUENCY REFERENCE (USA)                         compiled 2026-11-12
================================================================================
Compiled from FCC rules (47 CFR Part 95 / 97 / 80) and the sources listed at the
end. CHECK YOUR LICENCE before transmitting: some services need one (GMRS, amateur),
some are receive-only for you (marine/aviation/public safety outside an emergency).
Real distress (imminent danger to life): any means of communication is allowed.

RATSPEAK / RETICULUM LoRa (this project)
  915.000 MHz, BW 250 kHz, SF 11, CR 4/5 (T-Deck Pro default). 902-928 MHz ISM band,
  licence-free (Part 15). Bench units on unknown antennas run -9 dBm.
  Meshtastic US default (LongFast) 906.875 MHz - different modulation/settings.
