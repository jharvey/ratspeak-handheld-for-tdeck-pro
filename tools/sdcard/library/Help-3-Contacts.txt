HELP: ADD A CONTACT

New radios are not added to Contacts by themselves. You save one by giving it a name.

1. Both radios announce: H (Home), then N (Announce ID). Do it on both units.
2. P opens Peers: every radio heard, with signal (dBm) and minutes since it was heard.
3. Pick the new radio, Enter. The contact page opens: 1 Message, 2 Rename, 3 Share, 4 Map number.
4. Press 2, type a name, Enter. Now it is in Contacts (C). Peers marks saved radios with *.

Rename later: C, pick it, Enter, 2, type, Enter.

Name this radio: C, row 0 (MyID), Enter, 2, type, Enter.

Got a contact card ([card] Alice in a chat)? Open that chat and press Enter on the empty message line to save it.

Move all your contacts to another radio: on the new radio, setup wizard step 9, 1 (Listen). On the old one: M, S, 6, row 7 Share all contacts, pick the new radio. Each contact arrives as a card and is saved.
Or by SD card: M, S, 6, row 8 Export writes ratdeckpro/contacts.txt; on the other radio row 9 Import reads it (one "name<TAB>address" per line).
