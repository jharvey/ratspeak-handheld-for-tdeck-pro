HELP: THE MAP (ATLAS)

Open it with A on any screen, or M then A. On a list row, A shows that contact's last position. On the map, A does nothing (a slip while panning is harmless).

K = show / hide the keys card (also the "Nav keys" tab at the bottom)

E S X F = move up / left / down / right, half a screen (a diamond around D)
D or Enter = centre on me
Z = zoom in, W = zoom out (wider)
N = next contact, L = locate a place
P = next waypoint, Alt+P = add a waypoint, Alt+SYM twice = delete it (Help-7)
M = menu, ^ = close the map
G = big arrow + distance to the selected contact or waypoint (or the track-back point). Moving, the arrow is relative to where you are going; standing, north is up. G or ^ goes back.
B = track back: follow your recorded track back to where it started (B again stops).
C I H leave the map for Contacts, Inbox, Home. Peers from the map: M then P.

Top line: zoom, centre, scale bar; * means the map is not following you. Bottom line: your GPS position, or the last fix's age and what the GPS is doing:
"fix 28m old, GPS on 45s 3/9 sats" (searching), "GPS next 12m" (off until the next try).
In Full and Balanced, opening the map turns the GPS on for a fix. The saving modes keep
their schedule and show the last fix (Custom: S.4.7 "Map wakes GPS").

Contacts' last shared positions show as numbered rings. A thick ring is under an hour old.
N steps through them, newest first: "#1 Alice 12km NE 8m ago".
Type a number and Enter to open that contact's chat.

Give a contact a number: C, pick, Enter, 4 (Map number), type 1-99, Enter.

Waypoints and places: see Help-7.
