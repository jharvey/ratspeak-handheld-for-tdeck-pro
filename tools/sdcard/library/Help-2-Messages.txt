HELP: SEND A MESSAGE

1. I opens the Inbox: your chats first, then every radio heard.
2. Pick a row: T / V, or hold SYM and type its number. Then Enter.
3. Type the message (Alt + key = other case, SYM = digits).
4. Enter sends it.
5. ^ goes back to the list.

Short: I, pick, type, Enter.

If the other radio is out of range, the message waits in a queue and goes out by itself when that radio is heard again.

Long list? L, type part of the name, Enter. ^ clears the filter.

The idle screen shows how many messages are unread.

RELAY FOR OTHERS
A unit can pass messages on for units that cannot hear each other: Settings, 8 (LoRa), last
row "Relay for others": Off, On USB power (only while plugged in), or Always (more battery).
Put the relaying unit high up, between the others. It forwards their announces and messages;
they need nothing set. Messages stay end-to-end encrypted: the relay cannot read them.
