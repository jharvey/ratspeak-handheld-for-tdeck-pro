HOME ASSISTANT VOICE

The unit can be a voice device for Home Assistant (HA) over WiFi. To HA it looks like an
ESPHome voice device. Press MIC, speak, pause: HA hears it, runs the command and its spoken
answer plays on the speaker.

ON THE UNIT
1. Join your home WiFi: Settings S.3 (WiFi), Scan networks.
2. Settings S.3, row 7 "Home Assistant voice" opens its page:
   1 Voice for Home Assistant: Enter turns it on. The first time, it makes an
     encryption key (rows 2 and 3).
   2-3 Encryption key: 44 characters, ending with "=". HA asks for this key.
     Enter on row 2 to type your own key instead (for example one HA or ESPHome made).
   4 New random key (Enter twice). HA then needs the new key.
   5 No encryption (Enter twice): HA connects without a key. Not recommended.
   6 Name in Home Assistant: the device name HA shows, port 6053.
   7 Wake word: Enter steps Off (MIC to talk) -> On USB power only -> Always.
     Off by default. See WAKE WORD below.
   8 Sensors for HA: Off (default) / Battery / Battery + position. HA then shows the
     unit's battery %, and with position its latitude and longitude, as sensors on the
     device page (HA reconnects to the unit when this changes).
   Row 1 then shows what it is doing: waiting for Home Assistant, ready: press MIC
   to talk, listening, thinking, speaking.

IN HOME ASSISTANT (once)
1. Settings, Devices & services. HA should find a new ESPHome device with the unit's name.
   Click Configure. If it does not appear: Add integration, ESPHome,
   host = the unit's IP address (Settings S.3, row 2), port 6053.
2. HA asks for the encryption key: type the 44 characters from rows 2 and 3.
   (Console "hakey" prints it, for copy and paste.)
3. Open the new device: pick the Assist pipeline (speech-to-text, conversation agent,
   text-to-speech) it should use.

TALKING
- Press MIC on any screen (not while typing a message) and say the command. With the wake
  word off (the default), "OK Nabu" alone does nothing.
- HA stops listening when you pause; MIC again stops it at once. Up to 15 s per command.
- A message at the bottom shows what HA heard and answered.
- MIC alone still does a full screen refresh when HA voice is off or not connected.

WAKE WORD
- With row 7 on, the unit sends its microphone to HA all the time and HA listens for the
  wake word (for example "OK Nabu" or "Hey Jarvis"). Say it, then the command: a short beep
  says HA heard the wake word. After the answer it listens for the wake word again.
- HA does the listening: its Assist pipeline needs a wake word engine (for example the
  openWakeWord add-on), and the device's settings in HA pick which wake word. Without one,
  row 1 shows HA's error and the unit tries again after 5 s, then less often (up to 1 min).
- MIC still works while it listens: it starts a command at once.
- "On USB power only" listens only while USB power is in. "Always" also listens on battery:
  WiFi and the microphone stay on and the unit never sleeps, so the battery lasts hours,
  not days. It does not listen while the unit is locked or during a walkie-talkie call.
- Console: "hawake" shows the setting, "hawake 0|1|2" sets it.

ANNOUNCEMENTS
- HA can announce to the unit (Assist satellite "announce" action): the text shows at the
  bottom with a chime. The unit cannot play HA's audio file (no MP3 player), so the words
  are shown, not spoken. During quiet hours (S.9, last row) there is no chime.

NOTES
- WiFi only: voice is too big for LoRa.
- While HA is connected the unit does not sleep (it must answer HA), so the battery
  goes down faster. Turn it off (S.3 row 7, row 1) when you do not need it.
- Too quiet or too loud for HA: console "micgain 1-32" (default 4).
