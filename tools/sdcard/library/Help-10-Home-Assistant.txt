HOME ASSISTANT VOICE

The unit can be a voice device for Home Assistant (HA) over WiFi. Hold MIC, speak, let go:
HA hears it, runs the command and its spoken answer plays on the speaker.

ON THE UNIT
1. Join your home WiFi: Settings S.3 (WiFi), Scan networks.
2. Settings S.3, row 7 "Home Assistant voice": Enter turns it on.
   The row then shows what it is doing:
   - waiting for Home Assistant: HA has not connected yet
   - ready: hold MIC to talk
   - listening / thinking / speaking

IN HOME ASSISTANT (once)
1. Settings, Devices & services. HA should offer a new "Wyoming Protocol" device named after
   the unit. Click Configure / Add.
   If it does not appear: Add integration, Wyoming Protocol,
   host = the unit's IP address (Settings S.3, row 2), port = 10700.
2. Open the new device: pick the Assist pipeline (speech-to-text, conversation agent,
   text-to-speech) it should use.

TALKING
- Hold MIC on any screen (not while typing a message), say the command, let go.
- Up to 15 s per command. A message at the bottom shows what HA heard and answered.
- MIC alone still does a full screen refresh when HA voice is off or not connected.

NOTES
- WiFi only: voice is too big for LoRa.
- While HA is connected the unit does not sleep (it must answer HA), so the battery
  goes down faster. Turn HA voice off (S.3 row 7) when you do not need it.
- HA announcements (notify / announce to this satellite) also play on the speaker.
- Too quiet or too loud for HA: console "micgain 1-32" (default 4).
