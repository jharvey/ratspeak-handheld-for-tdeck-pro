HELP: STORAGE, SWITCH OFF AND SOLAR

For a radio that is put away for weeks or months and used now and then (an emergency radio in a bag, a car, a dorm room).

SWITCH OFF: M, S, 5 (Power), V down to row 13 "Switch off", Enter, Enter again.
The screen shows SWITCHED OFF and keeps showing it. The e-ink needs no power for that.

SWITCH ON: hold the TOP side button about 2 seconds, until BOOTING shows. A short press does nothing, so a bump in a bag does not start it. Start-up takes a minute or more.
If that does not work: press the BOTTOM (restart) button, then within 3 seconds hold the top button 2 seconds.

WHILE IT IS OFF
It does not send or receive. Messages sent to you meanwhile are not kept. Tell your people when you will switch on (for example every evening at 8), or use Check-in mode (Help-9) when you are switched on.
Everything is powered down. How much it still uses is not measured yet; the aim is months on a full battery.
Twice a day it looks at the battery for a moment. Nearly empty: the screen changes to BATTERY EMPTY and the battery is cut off until USB power arrives.

CHARGING AND SOLAR
A USB charger, a power bank or a small 5 V USB solar panel on the USB socket charges the battery. It does NOT switch the radio on.
A solar panel only charges well in direct sun, facing the sun, not behind glass. A 5 W panel (about the size of this radio or a little larger) refills an hour of use in roughly an hour or two of good sun. Not measured with this radio yet.
Best for long storage: charge it fully, switch it off, and top it up every few months.

USING IT FOR AN HOUR A DAY
Switch on, wait for the clock and the GPS (open sky; the first fix after a long time off can take several minutes).
Map: M, then Map. Your position needs GPS; the maps are on the card and need no network.
When you are done: switch off again (M, S, 5, row 13). Do not just leave it on the idle screen: there it keeps listening and the battery lasts a day or two, not months.
To use less while it is on: Eco or Saver mode (M, S, 5), WiFi off.
An hour of use takes very roughly 5 to 8 % of the battery.

AFTER A LONG TIME OFF
The clock keeps running while it is off. If the battery went completely empty the clock is wrong until the GPS has a fix.
The Power page, row "Last time", shows how long it was off and the battery voltage before and after.
