HELP: SETUP AND BATTERY

First setup: a new radio starts the setup wizard (name, time zone, 12 or 24 h clock, energy mode, idle screen, password, import contacts). Enter = next, ^ = back, 0 = skip. Run it again: M, S, 6, row 6.
Afterwards: Look: M, S, 3, 1 (Theme). WiFi (optional): M, S, 2. Meet another radio: see Help-3.

Battery: M, S, 4 (Power).
Full: everything on, GPS on.
Balanced: day to day; GPS looks every 15 min.
Saver: longest; GPS hourly, no WiFi or key light.
Custom: pick each setting. (Also Ultra-low, On demand, Check-in.)
Keys and incoming messages stay instant in every mode. On USB power it runs Full anyway.

No GPS fix in Balanced or Saver? Go outside and wait up to 15 min, or switch to Full for a while.

Idle screen (M, S, 3, 2, row 8): Splash, Map, Library page or If found. All show the time, battery, date and unread messages at the top. The e-ink keeps the last picture with no power at all, so an If found screen stays readable on a flat battery.

Ghosting on the screen? MIC refreshes it.
