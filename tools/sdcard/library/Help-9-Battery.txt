HELP: SETUP AND BATTERY

First setup: a new radio starts the setup wizard (name, time zone, 12 or 24 h clock, energy mode, idle screen, password, import contacts). Enter = next, ^ = back, 0 = skip. Run it again: M, S, 7, row 6.
Afterwards: Look: M, S, 4, 1 (Theme). WiFi (optional): M, S, 3. Meet another radio: see Help-3.

Battery: M, S, 5 (Power).
Full: everything on, GPS on.
Balanced: day to day; GPS always on, in its own power-save.
Saver: longest; GPS hourly, no WiFi or key light.
Custom: pick each setting. (Also Ultra-low, On demand, Check-in.)
Keys and incoming messages stay instant in every mode. On USB power it runs Full anyway.

No GPS fix? Go outside with a view of the sky and wait a few minutes. In Saver the GPS looks only hourly: switch to Full for a while.
WiFi joined, or the ATAK feed on Bluetooth, keeps the radio awake in every mode: about a day of battery. The Power page says "awake: WiFi".

Idle screen (M, S, 4, 2, row 8): Splash, Map, Library page or If found. All show the time, battery, date and unread messages at the top. The e-ink keeps the last picture with no power at all, so an If found screen stays readable on a flat battery.

Time left: M, S, 6, row 11 ("about 34 h in Balanced"), also on the idle screen (~34h). It is measured from the real drain after 30 min on battery.

Low-battery notice: M, S, 6, row 12. Off by default. Pick the %, and who gets it: once per discharge they get "Battery dying (9%)" with your last position.

Ghosting on the screen? MIC refreshes it.
