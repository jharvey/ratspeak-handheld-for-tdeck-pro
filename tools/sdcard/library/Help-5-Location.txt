HELP: SHARE YOUR LOCATION

In a chat, MIC opens the location menu (MIC or ^ closes it):

1 Share my location: sends your position once. Without a fresh GPS fix it sends the last fix and its age.
2 Auto-share location: Enter steps off, every 5, 15, 30, 60 min.
3 Auto-share for: 1 h, 4 h or until off.
4 Stop all auto-sharing.
5 Show them on the map.

While auto-share is on, the chat header shows [loc 15m] and the top bar a small pin.

A position you receive shows as [loc]. Enter on the empty message line opens it on the map.

Arrival alerts: M, S, 8, 5. "Tell me when Alice is within 500 m of me (or of a waypoint)": checked each time her position arrives; a beep and a message. Once, or every time she comes back.

Range test: M, S, 7, 6, pick the other radio (it does the same). Both ping each other and log signal strength and GPS position to the SD card (ratdeckpro/rangetest). The screen shows pings, replies and lost ones; 1-4 sets 10/15/30/60 s, ^ stops. On a PC, tools/rangetest/rangetest_map.py turns the file into a map for Google Earth.

GPS needs open sky. Indoors it often gets no fix.
