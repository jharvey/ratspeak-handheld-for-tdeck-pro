HELP: SHARE A CONTACT

Send someone's address to another person so they can message them.

1. C, pick the contact, Enter. (Row 0 MyID shares this radio.)
2. Press 3 (Share).
3. The list says "Send <name> to:". Pick who gets it, Enter. ^ cancels.

It goes as a normal message any LXMF app can read: "Contact card: Alice" and the address.

On a RatSpeak T-Deck Pro the chat shows [card] Alice. Enter on the empty message line saves it as a contact.

Messages to a new contact may wait in the queue until that radio announces or is heard.
