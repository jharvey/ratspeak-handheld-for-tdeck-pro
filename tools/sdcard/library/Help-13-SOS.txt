SOS, QUICK REPLIES AND NOTES

SOS
1. Hold the TOP side button for 3 seconds.
2. The radio buzzes and counts down: "SOS in 5 s - any key cancels".
   Any key, or a short press of the button, stops it. Nothing is sent.
3. After 5 s it sends "SOS! <your name> needs help." with your position
   (GPS, last fix or saved position) and battery %.
4. Then a position update every 2 minutes, the full SOS every 10 minutes,
   also after a restart.
5. Hold the top button 3 s again to end it: "SOS cancelled" is sent.

WHO GETS IT
- All saved contacts (at most 8), or one group.
- M, S, Alerts, row 7 "SOS sends to": Enter steps through them.
- Alerts row 8 "SOS button (hold 3 s)": OFF = a long hold is just a
  press (lock). A running SOS can still be ended with a hold.
- In Check-in mode LoRa turns on for the SOS; no waiting for a window.

GETTING AN SOS
- The radio wakes up and sounds a loud alarm every 3 s for a minute,
  even in quiet hours or with the beep off. Any key silences it.
- The sender shows on the map (Atlas) at the position in the SOS.

QUICK REPLIES
- In a chat: MIC, then Q. A digit sends at once:
  1 OK  2 Yes  3 No  4 On my way  5 Where are you?
  6 Call me  7 I'm safe  8 Wait for me
- Any other key closes the list. Works in group chats too.

NOTES
- M, R (Library), last row "New note": type, Enter saves it.
- Saved on the SD card as Note-<date>-<time>.txt; it opens like any
  other file in the Library.

GOOD TO KNOW
- A short press of the top button still locks the radio; it now acts
  when you let go, so a long hold does not lock first.
- Over LoRa an SOS to 8 contacts takes a while to reach them all; one
  out of range gets it from the queue when it is heard again.
