RatSpeak T-Deck Pro: instructional videos (the learning series)

What this folder is
- Longer, guided videos that teach the device from the box to everyday use, one topic per
  video, in the order a new owner learns it. The short how-to clips stay in docs/videos/
  (one task each); these videos may reuse them as parts.
- Each video becomes a text guide later (same name, .md), so every script step must be
  something a reader can follow without the video.
- Re-run when features change: each entry lists the firmware areas it shows, so a change
  there means that video needs a remake.
- File names: NN_Name.mp4 (NN = order in the series), _ instead of spaces.
- Same conventions as docs/videos/Video_Descriptions.txt (hand pointer and click sounds,
  SYM / Alt presses, overlay boxes, start and end on the idle screen, two units side by side
  with name labels).
- Status per entry: PLANNED (listed here), SCRIPTED (full steps written), MADE (mp4 here).


==========================================================================================
THE CAST (a family of rats, so the series is fun to watch)
==========================================================================================
Original characters (not Remy or other film rats: those belong to the studios, and these
videos will be public). Colours are the name tags over each unit: no red or blue.
- PIP WHISKERTON (person 1, green): a young rat with his first radio. Eager, a bit clumsy,
  learns everything the viewer learns.
- MABEL CHEDDARWORTH (person 2, orange): a seasoned trail rat, Pip's older cousin. Knows
  every key, explains with a wink.
- PROFESSOR NIBBLES (person 3, light purple): an absent-minded old scholar rat. Loves long
  explanations, wanders off, forgets where he left things.
- GRANNY GOUDA (when a 3rd or 4th is needed): wants it simple; reads big letters only.
- Running jokes: "Is the cheese safe?" / "The cheese is always safe."; nobody trusts the
  WiFi network called MouseTrap_5G; the cat is never seen, only reported.
- Real places with a cheesy or ratty name used as GPS positions: Plymouth, Wisconsin (the
  Cheese Capital of the World), Cheddar Gorge (England), Gouda (Netherlands), Hamelin
  (Germany, the Pied Piper town), Rat Island (Alaska), the Paris Sewer Museum.
- Overlay boxes may speak in the cast's voice ("Pip: wait, which key is Back?"), but the
  step itself is always stated plainly, so the text guide made from the video still reads
  as instructions.

THE SERIES

-- 01_Unbox_to_first_conversation.mp4                                    PLANNED (long)
- Purpose: from a new unit in the box to a first two-way conversation.
- Theme: Pip's first radio arrives in a cheese crate in Plymouth, Wisconsin. He sets it
  up, swaps contacts with Mabel, and their first conversation is "Is the cheese safe?" /
  "The cheese is always safe."
- Parts: what is in the box and the keys; charging over USB-C; putting RatSpeak on it (web
  flasher); first start (BOOTING banner) and the setup wizard; the home screen and your
  address; swapping contacts with a second unit (Pip and Mabel); first message and reply;
  back to the idle screen. Ends with the battery calibration note: charge full, run it until
  it switches itself off, charge full again.
- Reuses: New_out_of_box_unit_first_turn_on, Contact_exchange, send / read message clips.
- Shows: wizard, Contacts, contact card share, chat, idle screen, battery.

-- 02_Everyday_messaging_Minimal_mode.mp4                                PLANNED
- Purpose: daily use with the fewest keys: wake, read, reply, back to sleep.
- Theme: Granny Gouda in Gouda, Netherlands: Pip asks "Coming for Sunday supper?", she
  wakes the unit, reads it and replies "Bring crackers". Big, slow, simple.
- Parts: idle screen and the new-message count; any key wakes to the Inbox; open a chat,
  read, reply; delivered / not yet delivered; a message to someone out of range waits in
  the outbox; unlock with the password if set.
- Shows: Minimal mode, Inbox, chat, outbox, lock.

-- 03_Everyday_messaging_Everything_mode.mp4                             PLANNED
- Purpose: the same day with every feature showing: what each extra menu entry is for.
- Theme: Professor Nibbles gives a lecture tour of every menu, pointer in hand, getting
  carried away at each one ("and THIS, my dear students, is the Peers list...").
- Parts: the full menu (Home, Inbox, Contacts, Peers, Atlas, Library, Settings ...); chat
  menu (MIC): share location, voice message, contact card; Peers and announce; the home
  card; where each feature is explained further in this series.
- Shows: Everything mode, menu, chat menu, Peers.

-- 04_Configure_Typical_mode.mp4                                         PLANNED
- Purpose: choose what your unit shows: Minimal / Typical / Everything, and pick the Typical
- Theme: Mabel sets up Granny Gouda's unit: turns off what Granny will never use, keeps
  messages and If found, picks big idle screen.
  features one by one.
- Parts: Settings 1 Mode; Typical features list (turn each on / off and see the menu
  change); touch on / off; idle screen choice and timeout.
- Shows: Settings Mode, Typical features, Screen & I/O.

-- 05_Energy_modes.mp4                                       PLANNED (long: may be 3 parts)
- Purpose: make the battery last: what each energy mode does and how to pick or tune one.
- Theme: The Long Winter Burrow: Pip is snowed in for a week and rations battery like
  cheese. Each energy mode is a "cheese ration" (Full power = the whole wheel in a day,
  Ultra-low = one crumb an hour). Part C: Mabel teaches battery care.
- Part A: the modes (Full power, Balanced, Saver, Ultra-low, On demand): what changes
  (radio listening, GPS, screen, wake time) and the hours each gives on the Power page.
- Part B: Custom power: each setting and its cost; the energy monitor and the energy trend
  graph; "time left" and the learned battery capacity.
- Part C: battery care: charging, the calibration cycle, the low-battery notice, the
  BATTERY EMPTY banner and why it switches itself off, BOOTING when USB comes back.
- Shows: Settings 5 Power, Custom power, Energy monitor / trend, 6 Battery, Low-battery.

-- 06_Home_Assistant_mode.mp4                                            PLANNED
- Purpose: use the handheld with Home Assistant: alerts from the house, commands to it,
- Theme: Rat Manor, the smart burrow: Pip says "open the pantry door"; the house warns
  "Cat detected at the back door"; Mabel arrives home and the burrow knows.
  voice.
- Parts: what is needed at home (the bridge); Settings Home Assistant voice; sending a
  command; receiving an alert; presence (home / away). Only what is built at recording
  time; the rest is said as "coming".
- Shows: HA voice page, chat with the house, alerts.

-- 07_Atlas_maps_and_navigation.mp4                                      PLANNED (long)
- Purpose: the offline map: move around, find places, mark and go to them.
- Theme: The Great Cheese Hunt in Cheddar Gorge, England: waypoints are cheese caves, the
  breadcrumb track is (literally) a trail of crumbs, and an arrival alert fires when Pip
  reaches the Big Cave.
- Parts: open the Atlas, pan / zoom / keys (K), places search, your GPS position; contacts
  on the map; waypoints (save, step through); breadcrumb track (record, show, track back,
  GPX on SD); navigate to a contact or waypoint (arrow + distance); arrival alerts; map
  layers. Reuses atlas.mp4.
- Shows: Atlas, waypoints, track, navigate, arrival alerts, map packs on SD.

-- 08_Finding_people_and_contacts.mp4                                    PLANNED
- Purpose: get people into your contacts and keep them tidy.
- Theme: The Rat Pack reunion: Pip announces, old friends show up in Peers one by one, he

- Parts: announce and what it does; Peers (who has announced); peer to contact; naming a
  contact; contact cards (send / receive); removing a contact; your own identity card and
  address. Reuses peer_search, add_contact, contact_exchange.

-- 09_Voice_messages_and_walkie_talkie.mp4                               PLANNED
- Purpose: talk instead of type. Needs two real units (sound).
- Theme: Pip and Mabel in the tunnels under Paris (the Sewer Museum): walkie-talkie

- Parts: record and send a voice message, play one; walkie-talkie call: call, answer, talk
  / listen with Space, hang up; same WiFi or the unit's own hotspot; range and battery

- Shows: chat MIC 5, contact card W, walkie screen.

-- 10_Location_sharing_and_team_awareness.mp4                            PLANNED
- Purpose: let your group see where you are, safely.
- Theme: The Rat Island (Alaska) expedition: the team shares positions, the Professor is

- Parts: share my location once; auto-share and the "sharing on" sign; seeing a contact's
  last position and its age; navigate to them; check-in schedule; low-battery notice with

- Shows: chat MIC 1 / 2, Atlas contacts, Check-in, Low-battery notice.

-- 11_Lock_If_found_and_privacy.mp4                                      PLANNED
- Purpose: protect the unit and get it back if lost.
- Theme: Hamelin, Germany: the Pied Piper plays, Pip follows the music and drops his

- Parts: set a password, lock, unlock; the If-found idle screen and its details; what a
  finder sees; what the lock protects (and what not yet).
- Shows: Screen & I/O idle splash, If found page, lock.

-- 12_WiFi_and_internet_links.mp4                                        PLANNED
