RatSpeak T-Deck Pro: video descriptions

Scripts for the how-to videos. Each entry is one video. The videos are made by someone else
from these scripts, are later turned into text how-to documents for new users, and the
scripts are re-run whenever a feature changes, so every step must be clear and repeatable.
Keys checked against docs/HowTo.md, AI-guide.txt and the firmware menus on 16 Nov 2026.
A step marked "(check keys before recording)" could not be confirmed from the docs.


==========================================================================================
CONVENTIONS FOR EVERY VIDEO
==========================================================================================

- File names: use _ instead of a space, to prevent %20 issues in web pages etc.
- Every entry has the same parts: the file name line ("-- Name.mp4"), Purpose (what the
  viewer learns), Setup (units, names, idle screen, password, location / GPS, contacts
  already loaded), Steps (numbered, keys named exactly), Overlays (when not given inline in
  the steps) and End.
- Start on the idle screen and end on the idle screen. Any key wakes the unit. A unit wakes
  to the screen used last (in these videos usually the Inbox, because that is what most
  people used last); in Minimal mode it always wakes to the Inbox.
- Units on screen: either one unit, shown large, or two units side by side, each with a
  label above it showing its name (for example "JACK" left, "JILL" right).
- Hand pointer with click sounds on every key press; the key's name shows above the
  keyboard as it is pressed.
- Typing is shown in ALL CAPS (the keyboard types capitals). Alt + key gives a small letter.
- Digits: when showing a number being pressed, show one hand press and hold "SYM" with a half
  click sound. Then the other hand presses the key with a full click sound. Then SYM is
  released with the 2nd half click sound. Alt + key (small letters) is shown the same way.
  The digit keys with SYM held: W E R = 1 2 3, S D F = 4 5 6, Z X C = 7 8 9, MIC = 0.
- Other keys used in the scripts: ^ (Shift tapped alone) = Back, Enter = open / send / save,
  T / V = up / down in lists, M = Menu, I = Inbox, C = Contacts, P = Peers, A = Atlas.
  In Settings a row's number opens that row at once; in lists the number selects the row
  and Enter opens it. While typing a message the letter keys type letters: press ^ first
  to use them as keys again.
- Overlay boxes: short (one or two lines), shown long enough to read twice, placed where
  they do not hide the part of the screen being explained. An arrow or a hand wave may point
  at the part of the screen being talked about.
- The names, places and messages in Setup must match what is on the screen, so the same
  video can be made again after a firmware change.

SCRIPTED VIDEOS (in the order a new owner needs them)

-- New_out_of_box_unit_first_turn_on.mp4

Purpose: show a new owner what to expect, from the box to the home screen, plus the first
battery calibration.

Setup:
- One unit, shown large, with a PC (or laptop) on the left of the frame.
- The unit is fresh out of the box: screen blank or showing the LilyGO factory demo.
- No SD card needed. No contacts. No password.

Steps:
1. Overlay box: "A new T-Deck Pro: first we put RatSpeak on it".
2. Plug the USB-C cable into the unit and the PC. Close-up of the side of the unit.
3. Show the hand press and hold the BOOT button on the side, tap the RESET button, then let
   go of BOOT (half click sounds as in the SYM presses). Overlay box: "Hold BOOT, tap RESET,
   let go of BOOT: the unit is now ready to receive firmware". (Check the button names and
   positions against the LilyGO T-Deck Pro guide before recording.)
4. Cut to the PC: the RatSpeak web flasher in the browser (ratspeak.org/download.html).
   Pick "T-Deck Pro", pick the serial port, Flash. Show the progress bar filling, then
   "Done". Overlay box: "About 1 minute. Keep the cable in until it says done".
5. Back to the unit: tap RESET (or unplug and plug in again). The screen shows the idle
   screen with the "BOOTING v0.1" banner at the bottom. Overlay box: "First start takes a
   few seconds".
6. The setup wizard opens: Welcome, then one step at a time, Enter to go on:
   - Device name: type a name, e.g. "TRAILHEAD".
   - Time zone: pick yours.
   - Clock.
   - Energy mode: Balanced.
   - Idle screen: pick If found (its name / phone lines are filled in later in Settings).
   - Simple / Advanced: Typical.
   - Password lock: skip for now.
   - Import contacts: skip.
   Hand pointer with click sounds on each key, ALL CAPS typing.
7. At the last step, overlay box: "0 (SYM + MIC) finishes the wizard at any step;
   everything can be changed later in Settings".
8. The home screen appears: the identity card with the new name and address. Overlay box:
   "This is your address: others add you with it, or you announce so they find you (see
   Search_for_others.mp4)".
9. Battery calibration (first full cycle). The battery % is only a rough guess on a new
   unit (one unit showed 62% when it was really full). Show it in three short scenes:
   a. Charge to full: leave it on USB until the battery icon / battery page (Settings, 6
      Battery) says full or charged. This takes a few hours; use a wall charger or a PC port
      that really charges, not a data-only port or hub. Overlay box: "Step 1: charge it
      full. The unit learns what full looks like".
   b. Run it down: unplug and use it normally (or leave it on) until it switches itself off
      with the "BATTERY EMPTY / PLUG IN A USB CHARGER" banner on the idle screen. Time-lapse
      with a clock in the corner. Overlay box: "Step 2: use it until it switches itself off.
      Do not top it up part way".
   c. Charge it to full again without using it. Overlay box: "Step 3: charge it full again.
      From now on the % and time left are much more accurate".
   d. Closing overlay box: "Do this once with a new unit, and again if the % starts to look
      wrong".

Overlays: inline in the steps.

End: the unit goes to the idle screen (the If found screen just picked in the wizard).


-- Common_checkup.mp4

Purpose: the everyday check-up: wake the unit, unlock it, read a new message and answer it.

Setup:
- Two units side by side: "AUTHOR" on the left, "JILLIAN" on the right, each labeled.
- Both on the RatSpeak (Splash) idle screen. Author has a password lock with the password
  "42". Author and Jillian are already contacts of each other.
- Author's Inbox has at least 3 chats; Jillian's chat is the 3rd row and has one new
  message from Jillian: "WHAT IS THE ANSWER TO THE ULTIMATE QUESTION OF LIFE, THE UNIVERSE
  AND EVERYTHING?"

Steps:
1. Emphasize the new message on Author's idle screen somehow: wave a hand near it, or use an
   arrow pointing at the new-message count at the top of the idle screen.
2. Author presses a key. The unit asks for the password: type 4 (SYM + S), 2 (SYM + E),
   then Enter.
3. The Inbox opens. The 3rd row, Jillian's chat, is marked "1 new".
4. Press 3 (SYM + R) to select it, then Enter to open it. Show Jillian's question.
5. Author types the reply "42" (SYM + S, SYM + E) and presses Enter. "delivered" shows.
6. On Jillian's unit the answer arrives. Jillian replies: "DEEP THOUGHT HERE: CALCULATION
   COMPLETE. 7.5 MILLION YEARS WELL SPENT." (Embellish the Hitchhiker's Guide reference a
   bit if wanted, e.g. "NOW WHAT WAS THE QUESTION?")
7. The reply shows on Author's unit. Author acknowledges with "OK" and Enter.
8. Author presses ^ to go back to the Inbox.

Overlays:
- At step 1: "A new message is waiting".
- At step 8: "After a while the unit sleeps, and locks again, by itself".

End: Author's unit goes back to the idle screen by itself (locked).


-- Contact_exchange.mp4

Purpose: two people who meet swap contact cards, so they can message each other.

Setup:
- Side-by-side video: two units, "JACK" on the left and "JILL" on the right, each labeled.
- Both idle screens are the Map, at Cave Spring Hollow in Lynchburg, Tennessee. Zoom 14.
- No password on either unit. Both wake to the Inbox, as the Inbox is what they probably
  used last.
- Jack and Jill are not contacts yet, but both have announced, so each is in the other's
  Peers list.

Steps:
1. Start on both idle screens with the overlay box: "Jack meets Jill, and is going to send
   his contact to her".
2. Jack presses a key: it goes to the Inbox.
3. Jack presses C (Contacts). Row 0 at the top, MyID, is his own card: press Enter on it.
4. Jack presses 3 (SYM + R) = Share. The list reads "Send JACK to:".
5. Jill is not a contact yet, so Jack presses P to switch to Peers, picks Jill (T / V) and
   presses Enter. His card goes out.
   5.1 show arrow or similar to point 0 NEW on jill device. Which changes to 1 NEW.
6. Overlay box: "Jill receives his contact and sends her contact to Jack".
7. Jill presses a key: the Inbox shows a new chat from Jack. She opens it (its number, then
   Enter): it shows "[card] JACK". She presses Enter on the empty message line: Jack is
   saved as a contact.
8. Jill presses ^, then C: Jack is in her Contacts. She opens MyID (row 0) with Enter,
   presses 3 (SYM + R) = Share, picks Jack (now a contact) and presses Enter.
9. On Jack's unit: the Inbox shows a new chat from Jill with "[card] JILL". He opens it and
   presses Enter on the empty message line. Then ^ and C: show that Jill has been added to
   his contacts.

Overlays:
- At steps 1 and 6 as given above.
- At step 7: "Enter on the empty message line saves the card as a contact".
- At the end: "Jack and Jill can now message each other. Other LXMF apps see 'Contact card:
  name' and the address".

End: both units go back to the idle screen (the Map at Cave Spring Hollow).

(Note: an earlier version of this script used M P R to send the contact. M P R opens Peers
and announces the unit; it does not send a contact card. Sharing is C, MyID, Enter, 3.)


-- Search_for_others.mp4

Purpose: announce your unit so others can find you, find a new person in Peers, save them as
a contact and send a first message.

Setup:
- Two units side by side, each labeled: "COLUMBO" on the left, the person of interest on the
  right. Pick a character from one of the Columbo movies, e.g. "DR FLEMMING" (Dr. Ray
  Flemming, from "Prescription: Murder").
- Columbo's idle screen is If found, filled in with detective Columbo's details (e.g. LT.
  COLUMBO, LAPD HOMICIDE, LOS ANGELES, CA, plus a phone number).
- No password. Columbo's unit wakes to the Inbox. The two are not contacts yet.

Steps:
1. Columbo presses a key: it goes to the Inbox.
2. M, then P: Peers.
3. Overlay box briefly explains the purpose of announce (see Overlays). Columbo presses R to
   announce, so others can find him.
4. Overlay box briefly explains how people get into the Peers list.
5. On the right, Dr. Flemming's unit announces (P, then R).
6. On Columbo's unit a new ID shows up in Peers (press P again to refresh the list), which we
   know is a person of interest.
7. Overlay box: "Dr. Flemming just announced, and we know who this is: let's add him to
   the contacts".
8. Columbo picks the new row (T / V) and presses Enter: its contact page opens (1 Message,
   2 Rename, 3 Share, 4 Map number).
9. He presses 2 (SYM + E) = Rename, types "DR FLEMMING" and presses Enter. The peer is now a
   contact (Peers marks saved radios with *).
10. Overlay box: he will now send a confirmation message.
11. Enter again on the row, then 1 (SYM + W) = Message. He types "JUST ONE MORE THING..." and
    presses Enter. "delivered" shows; the message arrives on Dr. Flemming's unit.

Overlays:
- Step 3: "Announce tells radios nearby who you are, so they can find you and message you".
- Step 4: "Peers lists every radio that announced and was heard, with its signal and how
  long ago".
- Steps 7 and 10 as given above.

End: Columbo's unit goes back to the idle screen (If found).


-- Add_wifi_in_minimal_mode.mp4

Purpose: a unit in Minimal mode joins a WiFi network (Minimal hides the WiFi settings, so
the unit is switched to Typical first).

Setup:
- One unit, shown large. Minimal mode, no password.
- Idle screen: If found (the "return to" variant), with W1AW in CT as the address
  (ARRL HQ, 225 Main Street, Newington, CT 06111).
- Routers in range likely found near that ham station, with ham radio themed names, e.g.
  "ARRL-HQ" (the one to join, password HIRAMMAXIM), "W1AW-SHACK", "QST-GUEST",
  "NEWINGTON-73".

Steps:
1. Press a key: it goes to the Inbox (Minimal always wakes to the Inbox).
2. M, then S: Settings. In Minimal it shows only row 1, Mode.
3. Press 1 (SYM + W) = Mode, then 2 (SYM + E) = Typical. (WiFi settings are on in the
   Typical features, row 5, by default.)
4. Press ^ to go back to Settings: it now shows all rows.
5. Press 3 (SYM + R) = WiFi, then 3 again = Scan networks. The routers are listed.
6. Press the number of "ARRL-HQ".
7. Type the password HIRAMMAXIM (MIC shows or hides it) and press Enter. (Check keys
   before recording: the join page has Network / Password / Auto-connect / Join rows.)
8. "connected" shows on the WiFi page. The unit joins this network again by itself.
9. Optional: switch back to Minimal with M, S, 1, 1 (SYM + W twice). (Check before
   recording that WiFi stays connected in Minimal.)

Overlays:
- Step 2: "Minimal mode shows only the Mode row. Switch to Typical to see WiFi".
- Step 8: "WiFi is optional: messages go over LoRa radio without it".

End: the unit goes back to the idle screen (If found, W1AW).


-- atlas.mp4

Purpose: open the Atlas (offline map), move around, find a place, get the heading and
distance to it, and step through the contacts' last positions.

Setup:
- One unit, shown large. The SD card holds the maps (without them the menu has no Atlas).
- GPS position is "The Alamo" in San Antonio, Texas.
- Contacts already loaded with last shared positions at vacation spots: one at the Vietnam
  Veterans Memorial ("Vietnam wall") in Washington DC, a remote mountain hiker in Montana,
  and one at the Cutler VLF radio station in Maine.
- Unit wakes to the Inbox, as for most people the last thing they did was the Inbox.

Steps:
1. Start on the idle screen. Press a key: it comes off the idle screen to the Inbox.
2. Press A: the Atlas opens on your position (the Alamo).
3. Press K to show the card with the navigation keys. K again hides it.
4. Navigate around to show how it works: T F V H move the map up, left, down, right; W zooms
   out (wider), Z zooms in; D (or Enter) centres it on you again.
5. Locate Mount Rushmore: press L, type "MOUNT RUSH", Enter, then its number (1 = SYM + W)
   jumps there.
6. Press G: the heading and distance from your current position (about 1667 km, heading
   346). G again goes back to the map.
7. Press N to go through the contacts, newest first: the Vietnam wall in DC, the remote
   mountain hiker in Montana, and the VLF radio station in Maine.
8. Once done, press I to return to the Inbox.

Overlays:
- Step 3: "K shows all the map keys".
- Step 5: "L finds a place by name".
- Step 6: "G: heading and distance from you".
- Step 7: "N steps through your contacts' last shared positions".

End: the unit goes back to the idle screen.
