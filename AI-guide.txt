WHY PSRAM NOW
-------------
TX path works past Recovering, but MessageStore CreateOutgoing fails:
  outcome=1 (Failed) err=2 (Allocation) at free_int≈20K after lv_obj_clean.

[PSRAM] found=1 size=0 free=0 caps_spiram=0
  → chip ID seen, OPI PSRAM not mapped into the address space.
Without SPIRAM the node stays RS_HANDHELD_PROFILE_SMALL on INTERNAL only;
MessageStore cannot allocate outbound records under the e-ink/LVGL heap load.
