MESSAGE / TX CHECKLIST
----------------------
1. On Peers or Msg Box, ensure a row is selected (1> name or tap)
2. Serial should show [PEER] key/touch sel=N <hash>
3. Type message, Enter
4. Expect [TX] to <hash> body="..." then outcome=0 and [LORA_IF] TX
5. If [TX] no peer — nothing selected; pick 1-9 or tap first

Announce: Home Announce or key A → [ANN] / [LORA_IF] TX
Plus "5s" under RECENT PEERS = Pro announce heard recently (good).
If Plus does not show Pro messages: confirm Pro serial shows TX outcome=0
and dest hash matches Plus identity; both on same freq/preset.
