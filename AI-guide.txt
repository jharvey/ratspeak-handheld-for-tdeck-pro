HOW TO SELECT PEER + SEND
-------------------------
1. Open Inbox (touch bottom-left or key I) or Peers (P) or Contact (C)
2. Tap a row (or key N to cycle) until ">" marks the peer
3. Type message (leading space if it would start with A/S/H/I/C/P/V/N/T)
4. Pause ~1.6s to see footer, or just press Enter to send
5. Serial confirms [TX] outcome=0 and [LORA_IF] TX …

  H  Home              I  Inbox

  V  View thread       N  Next peer
  T  Test ping         Enter empty = next tab
