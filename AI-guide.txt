HEADER
[DONE] Refresh button (circular arrows) left of Settings — full e-ink refresh
       (same as mic). Touch left strip or mic key.

TIME ZONE
[DONE] Settings > Time > "1 Set time zone >" opens scrollable zone list
       (EST default, EDT/CST/.../UTC offsets). Enter selects; Back returns.

IDLE SPLASH
[DONE] After 60s idle (no compose): logo + HH:MM clock + battery + 
       "press SPACE to wake". Space/any key/touch wakes.
[DONE] Settings > Screen & Input > Idle splash submenu:
         No idle screen | Idle no password (default) | Idle + password TBD
       Password option is inert (TBD).

E-INK: partial-only UI; full only via refresh button or mic.
