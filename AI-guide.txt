BATTERY / BQ27220 "learn"
  Fuel-gauge chips estimate % from voltage + coulomb count. Until the pack has
  been through a real empty→full (or full→empty) cycle, RelativeStateOfCharge
  can sit far from true (e.g. 45% at 4.1V on USB). "Learn" = use the device
  until low, then charge uninterrupted to full so the gauge can recalibrate.
  Voltage-curve blend still corrects the worst USB cases.
