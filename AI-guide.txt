STANDALONE BUILD RULES
----------------------
env: tdeck_pro_standalone must:
  - extends tdeck_Pro_base
  - EXCLUDE core/runtime/LvglApplication.cpp
  - EXCLUDE ui/lvgl/ source compile (headers may still be on include path)
  - INCLUDE boards/tdeck_Pro/ (DisplayEink, LvglPort, LvglStubs, main)

LVGL custom symbols (from lv_conf.h):
  handheld_lvgl_alloc / free / realloc / memory_stats  → LvMemory.h
  handheld_lvgl_fail(void) / failure_display           → LvFailure.h
  lv_font_rsdeck_14                                    → stand-in montserrat_16 for bring-up
