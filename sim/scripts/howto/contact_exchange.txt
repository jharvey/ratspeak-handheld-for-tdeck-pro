# How-to video: Jack and Jill swap contact cards. Device hv_jack (recorded), companion
# contact_exchange_jill.txt (hv_jill, recorded too: the video shows both side by side).
# Idle screen: the map (tiles around the Alamo on both cards), no password.
expect 60000 [BOOT] ready
serial wiz skip
# wake it (a start ends on the idle screen): the first key does nothing else
wait 1500
key SPACE
wait 800
serial idlescr 1
wait 2000
serial a
expect 60000 {JILL16}
wait 3000
key M
key H
wait 1500
serial idle now
wait 2500
rec contact_exchange
note 5500 Jack meets Jill, and is going to send his contact to her.
wait 6000
caption Jack wakes his radio (any key) and opens Contacts (C). Row 0 is his own card, MyID: Enter.
wait 1500
key SPACE
wait 1500
key C
wait 1500
key 0
wait 800
key ENTER
wait 2000
caption 3 (Share). The list asks who gets it: P switches to Peers, where Jill's radio is. Enter sends.
wait 1500
key 3
wait 1800
key P
wait 1800
key ENTER
expect 20000 [CARD] sent
wait 2500
expect 120000 [MSG] inbound
wait 3000
caption Jack gets Jill's card: any key wakes his radio, I (Inbox), open her chat, Enter on the empty line saves it.
wait 1500
key SPACE
wait 1500
key I
wait 1500
key ENTER
wait 2500
key ENTER
wait 2500
caption C: Jill is in Jack's contacts now. They can message each other.
wait 1500
key BACK
wait 1200
key C
wait 4000
rec off
quit
