# How-to video: the Atlas map layers (O). Device hv_jack at Cave Spring Hollow, Lynchburg TN
# (seed "tn": map tiles and radio coverage tiles in maps/coverage).
gps on
expect 60000 [BOOT] ready
serial wiz skip
wait 1500
key SPACE
wait 800
serial maplayers 63
wait 3000
key I
wait 1500
serial idle now
wait 2500
rec Map_layers
caption Any key wakes the radio. A opens the Atlas.
wait 1500
key SPACE
wait 1500
key A
expect 8000 [MAP] open
wait 5000
caption O opens the map layers: names, contour lines, woods, hill shading, radio coverage ...
wait 1500
key O
expect 3000 [MAP] layers page
wait 5000
caption A layer's number turns it on or off. 7: the radio coverage overlay.
wait 1500
key 7
expect 3000 [MAP] layer Radio coverage on
wait 3000
caption ^ (Shift) goes back to the map, with the new layer drawn.
wait 1500
key BACK
wait 6000
caption O, 2 turns the contour lines off.
wait 1500
key O
wait 2000
key 2
expect 3000 [MAP] layer Contour lines off
wait 2500
key BACK
wait 6000
note 6000 The layers stay as set. The coverage overlay is made on a PC from elevation data (tools/maptiles/rf_coverage.py).
wait 6500
caption O, 2 and O, 7 put it back the way it was. ^ closes the map.
wait 1500
key O
wait 1500
key 2
wait 1500
key 7
wait 1500
key BACK
wait 3000
key BACK
expect 3000 [MAP] closed
wait 1500
key I
wait 1500
serial idle now
wait 8000
rec off
quit
