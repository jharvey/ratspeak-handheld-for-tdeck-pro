# How-to video: arrival alerts. Device hv_robin at the Alamo. Sage's shared positions are set
# with the console (cpos = as if they arrived over the radio): first 6 km away, then 30 m.
gps on
expect 60000 [BOOT] ready
serial wiz skip
# wake it (a start ends on the idle screen): the first key does nothing else
wait 1500
key SPACE
wait 800
serial geof clear
serial contact 5a9e0c2d7b41f6830e1d4c7a9b2f5e60 SAGE
serial cpos 5a9e0c2d7b41f6830e1d4c7a9b2f5e60 29.4700 -98.4500 3
wait 3000
key I
wait 1500
serial idle now
wait 2500
rec Arrival_alerts
note 6500 An arrival alert tells you when a contact comes near you, or near one of your waypoints. They must be sharing their location with you.
wait 7000
caption Any key wakes the radio. Settings: M, S, 9 (Alerts), then 5 (Arrival alerts).
wait 1500
key SPACE
wait 1500
key M
wait 900
key S
wait 1500
key 9
wait 1800
key 5
wait 2500
caption 1: New alert.
wait 1200
key 1
wait 2500
caption Row 1, Contact: who to watch. Each press of 1 goes to the next contact. Here: Sage.
wait 5000
caption Row 2, Near: me (where I am), or one of my waypoints. Each press of 2 goes to the next.
wait 5000
caption Row 3, Within: the distance. Press 3 until it is right: 500 m, 1 km, 2 km ...
wait 1500
key 3
wait 1500
key 3
wait 2500
caption Row 4, Repeat: once, or every time they come back. 4 changes it.
wait 1500
key 4
wait 2500
caption 5: Save alert. It is in the list now. (Enter twice on it removes it.)
wait 1500
key 5
expect 5000 [GEOF] new
wait 4000
caption Back to the Inbox. Sage is 6 km away: nothing happens.
wait 1500
key I
wait 4000
caption Later a position from Sage arrives that is inside the 2 km: the radio beeps and says so.
wait 2500
serial cpos 5a9e0c2d7b41f6830e1d4c7a9b2f5e60 29.4262 -98.4859 0
expect 5000 [GEOF] SAGE is
wait 1000
point 120 271 3500
wait 5000
serial idle now
wait 3500
rec off
quit
