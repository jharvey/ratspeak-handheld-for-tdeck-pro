# How-to video: change the operator mode and touch on / off. Device ht_alex.
expect 60000 [BOOT] ready
serial wiz skip
serial opmode 2
serial touchscreen 1
wait 3000
key M
key H
wait 1500
rec mode_touch
caption Open Settings (M, then S). Row 1 is Mode.
wait 1200
key M
wait 800
key S
wait 1800
caption Press 1 for Mode.
wait 1000
key 1
wait 2000
caption 1 Minimal: only Inbox, Contacts, talk. 2 Typical: the features picked on row 5. 3 Everything.
wait 2500
key 1
wait 2000
caption Minimal: the menu (M) shows only the basics.
wait 1000
key BACK
wait 1200
key M
wait 2800
caption To go back: Settings, 1, then 2 for Typical.
wait 1000
key S
wait 1200
key 1
wait 1200
key 2
wait 2000
caption Row 5: Typical features. Each one on or off: walkie-talkie, WiFi, Atlas, Peers ...
wait 1200
key 5
wait 3500
key BACK
wait 1500
caption Press 4 to turn the touch screen off (keys only). 4 again turns it on.
wait 1500
key 4
wait 2000
key 4
wait 2500
rec off
quit
