# How-to video: share your location. Device hv_robin (recorded) at the Alamo, companion
# Share_your_location_sage.txt (hv_sage, recorded too: both side by side), 1 km away.
gps on
expect 60000 [BOOT] ready
serial wiz skip
# wake it (a start ends on the idle screen): the first key does nothing else
wait 1500
key SPACE
wait 800
serial contact {SAGE} SAGE
wait 2000
serial a
expect 60000 {SAGE16}
wait 3000
key I
wait 1500
serial idle now
wait 2500
rec Share_your_location
note 6500 Nothing about where you are is sent unless you send it. Robin shares a position with Sage.
wait 7000
caption Robin wakes the radio and opens the chat with Sage: C (Contacts), 1, Enter, then 1 (Message).
wait 1500
key SPACE
wait 1500
key C
wait 1500
key 1
wait 900
key ENTER
wait 1800
key 1
wait 2500
caption In a chat, MIC opens the location menu.
wait 1500
key MIC
wait 3500
caption 1: Share my location. It goes to Sage once, as a message.
wait 1500
key 1
expect 30000 [LOC] share sent
wait 20000
caption MIC, then 2: Auto-share location. The radio sends your position every 5 minutes; 2 again: 15, 30, 60 minutes, off.
wait 1500
key MIC
wait 2000
key 2
expect 5000 [LOC] auto-share
wait 5000
note 6500 Row 3 sets for how long: 1 hour, 4 hours, or until you turn it off. To stop all sharing: Settings, 2 Time & Location, row 8.
wait 7000
caption ^ (Shift) leaves the chat. Later the radio sleeps again.
wait 1500
key BACK
wait 1200
key BACK
wait 1500
serial idle now
wait 12000
rec off
quit
