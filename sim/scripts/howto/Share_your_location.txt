# How-to video: share your location. Device hv_jack (recorded) at Cave Spring Hollow in
# Lynchburg, Tennessee, companion Share_your_location_jill.txt (hv_jill, recorded too: both
# side by side), about 1 km north. Both idle screens are the Map (seed "tn": zoom 3 to 13).
gps on
expect 60000 [BOOT] ready
serial wiz skip
# wake it (a start ends on the idle screen): the first key does nothing else
wait 1500
key SPACE
wait 800
serial idlescr 1
serial contact {JILL} JILL
wait 2000
serial a
expect 60000 {JILL16}
wait 3000
key I
wait 1500
serial idle now
wait 4000
rec Share_your_location
note 6500 Nothing about where you are, or your battery, is sent unless you send it. Jack shares where he is with Jill.
wait 7000
caption Jack's idle screen is the map: he is at Cave Spring Hollow. He wakes the radio and opens the chat with Jill: C, 1, Enter, then 1 (Message).
wait 2500
key SPACE
wait 1500
key C
wait 1500
key 1
wait 900
key ENTER
wait 1800
key 1
wait 2500
caption In a chat, MIC opens the share menu.
wait 1500
key MIC
wait 3500
caption 1 sends his position and battery once, as a message. B changes what is sent: both, position, or battery.
wait 1500
key 1
expect 30000 [LOC] share
wait 20000
caption MIC, then 2: automatic sharing. Every 5 minutes; 2 again: 15, 30, 60 minutes, off.
wait 1500
key MIC
wait 2000
key 2
expect 5000 [LOC] auto-share
wait 5000
note 6500 Row 3 sets for how long: 1 hour, 4 hours, or until you turn it off. To stop all sharing: Settings, 2 Time & Location, row 8.
wait 7000
caption ^ (Shift) leaves the chat. Later the radio sleeps again: back to the map.
wait 1500
key BACK
wait 1500
key BACK
wait 1500
serial idle now
wait 12000
rec off
quit
