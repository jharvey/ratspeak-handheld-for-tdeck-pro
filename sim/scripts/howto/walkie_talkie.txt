# How-to video: Jack calls Jill on the walkie-talkie. Device hv_jack (recorded), companion
# walkie_talkie_jill.txt (hv_jill, recorded too: both side by side). Neither is on a WiFi
# network, so Jack's radio makes its own hotspot and Jill's joins it (simulated WiFi,
# sim/hw/WifiNet.h). The simulated microphones play audio/wt_*.wav (Windows voices David =
# Jack, Zira = Jill); the video's sound has each voice and the Codec2 copy the other hears.
# Turn timing is shared with the companion: both count from "[WT] connected".
expect 60000 [BOOT] ready
serial wiz skip
wait 2000
serial a
expect 60000 {JILL16}
serial contact {JILL} Jill
wait 3000
key M
key H
wait 1500
serial idle now
wait 2500
rec walkie_talkie
note 6000 Jack wants to talk with Jill live. There is no WiFi out here: his radio makes its own.
wait 6500
caption Jack: any key wakes the radio, C (Contacts), pick Jill (1), Enter opens her card.
wait 1500
key SPACE
wait 1500
key C
wait 1500
key 1
wait 1000
key ENTER
wait 2500
caption W calls her. The invite goes over LoRa, and Jack's radio starts a small WiFi hotspot for the call.
wait 1500
key W
expect 60000 [WT] invite sent
expect 180000 [WT] connected
caption Connected. Space = talk, Space again = listen (one talks at a time). Jack talks first.
wait 1500
mic wt_jack1.wav
key SPACE
wait 5800
key SPACE
wait 9000
mic wt_jack2.wav
key SPACE
wait 5000
key SPACE
wait 8500
caption ^ hangs up. The hotspot closes and both radios go back to normal.
wait 1500
key BACK
expect 20000 [WT] hotspot / call WiFi released
wait 5000
rec off
quit
