# How-to video: a group. Device hv_robin (recorded) makes the group TRAIL CREW with Sage and
# Wren, shares it with Sage and writes to it. Companions: Group_messages_sage.txt (hv_sage,
# recorded too: side by side) joins and answers; Group_messages_wren.txt (hv_wren, not shown).
expect 60000 [BOOT] ready
serial wiz skip
# wake it (a start ends on the idle screen): the first key does nothing else
wait 1500
key SPACE
wait 800
serial contact {SAGE} SAGE
wait 2000
serial a
expect 60000 {SAGE16}
wait 3000
key I
wait 1500
serial idle now
wait 2500
rec Group_messages
note 6500 A group sends one message to everyone in it. Robin makes a group for the trail crew.
wait 7000
caption Robin wakes the radio: C (Contacts), then G: the groups.
wait 1500
key SPACE
wait 1500
key C
wait 1500
key G
expect 3000 [GRP] list
wait 2500
caption Enter on New group, type its name, Enter.
wait 1500
key ENTER
wait 1200
type TRAIL CREW
wait 1200
key ENTER
expect 3000 [GRP] new "TRAIL CREW"
wait 2500
caption Who is in it: Enter puts a contact in (Enter again takes it out). V moves down.
wait 1500
key ENTER
expect 3000 (1 members)
wait 2500
key BACK
wait 2500
caption The group's page. 4 shares the group with a member, so their radio has it too. Enter: Sage.
wait 2000
key 4
wait 2500
key ENTER
expect 30000 [GRP] share "TRAIL CREW" to SAGE
# give Sage time to get the three card parts and join before the first group message
wait 60000
# Wren joins the crew later (added here from the console: the video does not show it)
serial contact {WREN} WREN
wait 1500
serial grp add 1 {WREN}
expect 3000 (2 members)
wait 30000
note 5000 Wren has joined the crew too: the group now has Sage and Wren.
wait 5500
# the long wait let the screen go idle: this key only wakes it
key SPACE
wait 2000
caption 1: the group chat. Type, then Enter: it goes to Sage and Wren.
wait 1500
key 1
wait 2000
type MEET AT THE NORTH GATE
wait 1500
key ENTER
wait 4000
expect 400000 in from {SAGE}
wait 3000
# the screen went idle while waiting: a key wakes it back to the group chat
key SPACE
wait 3000
caption Sage's answer shows in the group chat, with her name in front.
wait 9000
note 6000 MIC in a group chat shows who got the last message. Later, the Inbox lists the group like a chat.
wait 6500
key BACK
wait 1500
key I
wait 1500
serial idle now
wait 12000
rec off
quit
