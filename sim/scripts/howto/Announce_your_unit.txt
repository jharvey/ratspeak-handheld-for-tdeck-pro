# How-to video: announce from Home (N) and from Peers (R). Device hv_robin; companion
# Announce_your_unit_sage.txt (hv_sage) announces so that Peers has someone in it.
expect 60000 [BOOT] ready
serial wiz skip
wait 3000
key I
wait 1500
serial idle now
wait 2500
rec Announce_your_unit
note 6500 Announce: your radio tells the radios nearby who it is. After that they can find you and message you.
wait 7000
caption Any key wakes the radio. Then M (Menu) and H (Home).
wait 1500
key SPACE
wait 1500
key M
wait 1200
key H
wait 2500
caption Home shows your name and your address: the two lines of letters and digits after LXMF.
point 150 60 3500
wait 4200
caption N: Announce ID. The radio sends its name and address once over LoRa.
wait 1500
key N
wait 3500
caption Others do the same. P opens Peers: every radio that announced and was heard.
expect 120000 {SAGE16}
wait 1500
key P
wait 3500
caption A new radio is in the list: the start of its address, its signal and how long ago. R on Peers announces too.
wait 2500
key R
wait 3500
note 6000 Announce once when you meet someone new, or when others cannot find you. It is not needed for every message.
wait 6500
caption I goes back to the Inbox. Later the radio sleeps again.
wait 1500
key I
wait 2000
serial idle now
wait 3500
rec off
quit
