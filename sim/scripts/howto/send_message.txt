# How-to video: send a message. Device ht_alex (contacts SAM, JO), with ht_sam running
# (companion_sam.txt) so the message is delivered. tools/howto/make_videos.ps1 runs it.
expect 60000 [BOOT] ready
serial wiz skip
# wake it (a start ends on the idle screen): the first key does nothing else
wait 1500
key SPACE
wait 800
serial contact {SAM} SAM
serial contact {JO} JO
wait 15000
key M
key H
wait 1500
rec send_message
wait 1200
caption Press I to open the Inbox. Your chats are at the top.
wait 1200
key I
wait 1800
caption Pick the chat with T (up) and V (down), or hold SYM and type its number. Here: SAM is row 1.
wait 1500
key V
wait 900
key T
wait 1000
caption Press Enter to open the chat.
wait 900
key ENTER
wait 1800
caption Type your message. Capitals are fine (Alt + key gives a small letter).
wait 1000
type ON MY WAY
wait 600
caption Press Enter to send it.
wait 900
key ENTER
wait 4000
caption Sent. If SAM is out of range it waits in a queue and goes out by itself. ^ (Shift) goes back.
wait 3000
key BACK
wait 2200
rec off
quit
