# How-to video: navigate to a contact. Device hv_robin at the Alamo, map tiles on its SD card.
# Sage's last shared position (6 km to the north-east, 7 minutes old) is set with the console.
gps on
expect 60000 [BOOT] ready
serial wiz skip
# wake it (a start ends on the idle screen): the first key does nothing else
wait 1500
key SPACE
wait 800
serial contact 5a9e0c2d7b41f6830e1d4c7a9b2f5e60 SAGE
serial cpos 5a9e0c2d7b41f6830e1d4c7a9b2f5e60 29.4700 -98.4500 7
wait 3000
key I
wait 1500
serial idle now
wait 2500
rec Navigate_to_a_contact
note 6000 Sage shared a position with Robin a few minutes ago. Robin wants to walk there.
wait 6500
caption Any key wakes the radio. M, then A opens the Atlas on your own position.
wait 1500
key SPACE
wait 1500
key M
wait 1200
key A
wait 3000
caption N steps through your contacts' last shared positions. The map goes to Sage.
wait 1500
key N
expect 5000 [MAP] contact
wait 3000
caption G: go. A big arrow, the distance, the direction, and how old Sage's position is.
wait 1500
key G
expect 5000 [NAV] open
wait 6000
note 7000 North is at the top. The radio has no compass: use the direction (NE, and the degrees) with a landmark, the sun or a compass.
wait 7500
caption G or ^ goes back to the map. D centres the map on you again.
wait 1500
key G
wait 2000
key D
wait 2500
caption I leaves the map for the Inbox.
wait 1200
key I
wait 2500
note 6500 A shortcut: with Sage's row selected in the Inbox or in Contacts, A opens the map at Sage's position at once.
wait 7000
serial idle now
wait 3500
rec off
quit
