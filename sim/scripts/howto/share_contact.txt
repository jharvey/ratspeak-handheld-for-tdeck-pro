# How-to video: send (share) a contact. Device ht_alex (contacts SAM, JO); companion_sam.txt.
expect 60000 [BOOT] ready
serial wiz skip
serial contact {SAM} SAM
serial contact {JO} JO
wait 15000
key M
key H
wait 1500
rec share_contact
caption Press C for Contacts.
wait 1200
key C
wait 2000
caption Pick the contact to send, here JO (row 2: SYM + E, or T / V). Enter opens it.
wait 1500
key 2
wait 1200
key ENTER
wait 2200
caption Press 3 (Share): hold SYM and press R.
wait 1500
key 3
wait 2200
caption Pick who gets the card: here T moves up to SAM. Then Enter. ^ cancels.
wait 2000
key T
wait 1200
key ENTER
expect 30000 [CARD] sent
wait 2500
caption Sent. SAM's chat shows [card] JO; Enter on the empty message line saves it there.
wait 3500
rec off
quit
