# Instructional video 20: out of range and back. Device hv_mabel (Mabel, recorded); companion
# 20_Out_of_range_and_back_pip.txt (hv_pip, recorded too) is started about a minute later:
# until then Pip's radio cannot be reached.
expect 60000 [BOOT] ready
serial wiz skip
# wake it (a start ends on the idle screen): the first key does nothing else
wait 1500
key SPACE
wait 800
serial contact {PIP} PIP
wait 3000
key I
wait 1500
serial idle now
wait 2500
rec 20_Out_of_range_and_back
note 7000 Pip has gone down the drainpipe. Down there his radio cannot hear Mabel's: he is out of range.
wait 7500
caption Mabel wakes her radio and writes to Pip: C (Contacts), 1, Enter, then 1 (Message).
wait 1500
key SPACE
wait 1500
key C
wait 1500
key 1
wait 900
key ENTER
wait 1800
key 1
wait 2500
type IS THE CHEESE SAFE?
wait 800
key ENTER
wait 4000
caption The message is sent, but no "delivered" comes back: Pip's radio did not hear it.
wait 9000
note 7500 Mabel does not have to send it again. The radio keeps the message and tries again by itself when Pip's radio is heard.
wait 8000
caption She leaves the chat open and waits ...
expect 240000 {PIP16}
wait 2000
caption Pip pops out of the pipe. His radio is heard again, and the waiting message goes out.
expect 240000 [MSG] inbound
wait 3000
caption Delivered, and Pip has answered: the cheese is always safe.
wait 9000
key BACK
wait 1500
serial idle now
wait 5000
rec off
quit
