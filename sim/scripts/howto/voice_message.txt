# How-to video: Jack answers Jill with a voice message. Device hv_jack (recorded), companion
# voice_message_jill.txt (hv_jill, recorded too: both side by side). The simulated microphone
# plays audio/vm_jack.wav (Windows voice "David", tools/howto/make_speech.ps1); what Jack says
# and what Jill's speaker plays are in the video's sound.
expect 60000 [BOOT] ready
serial wiz skip
# wake it (a start ends on the idle screen): the first key does nothing else
wait 1500
key SPACE
wait 800
wait 2000
serial a
expect 60000 {JILL16}
serial contact {JILL} Jill
wait 3000
mic vm_jack.wav
key M
key H
wait 1500
serial idle now
wait 2500
rec voice_message
note 5500 Jill asks where Jack is. He answers with a voice message.
expect 180000 [MSG] in from
wait 3000
caption Jack wakes his radio (any key), I (Inbox), and opens Jill's chat (Enter).
wait 1500
key SPACE
wait 1500
key I
wait 1500
key ENTER
wait 3000
caption In the chat: MIC opens the chat menu, 5 = Voice message.
wait 1500
key MIC
wait 1800
key 5
wait 2500
caption Space starts recording. Jack speaks (up to 20 seconds).
wait 1500
key SPACE
wait 8800
caption Space again stops and sends it over LoRa.
wait 800
key SPACE
expect 60000 [VM] sent
wait 3500
caption ^ closes the recorder. The voice message is in the chat.
wait 1500
key BACK
wait 90000
rec off
quit
