# How-to video: open the Atlas (offline map). Device ht_map (map tiles on its SD card,
# copied from the map master copy around the simulator's default GPS fix).
expect 60000 [BOOT] ready
serial wiz skip
wait 4000
key M
key H
wait 1500
rec atlas
caption Press A on any screen to open the Atlas, the offline map. (Also M, then A.)
wait 1500
key A
wait 3000
caption W zooms out (wider), Z zooms in.
wait 1000
key W
wait 1500
key W
wait 1500
key Z
wait 2000
caption T F V H move the map up, left, down, right.
wait 1000
key T
wait 1000
key F
wait 1000
key V
wait 1000
key H
wait 1200
caption D (or Enter) centres the map on you again.
wait 1000
key D
wait 2000
caption K shows the keys card with all map keys. K again hides it.
wait 1000
key K
wait 3500
key K
wait 1500
caption ^ (Shift) closes the map.
wait 1000
key BACK
wait 2200
rec off
quit
