# How-to video: markers (items of interest). Device hv_robin (recorded) at the Alamo,
# companion Markers_sage.txt (hv_sage, recorded too: both side by side), about 1 km away.
gps on
expect 60000 [BOOT] ready
serial wiz skip
# wake it (a start ends on the idle screen): the first key does nothing else
wait 1500
key SPACE
wait 800
serial contact {SAGE} SAGE
wait 2000
serial mark del all
serial a
expect 60000 {SAGE16}
wait 3000
key I
wait 1500
serial idle now
wait 2500
rec Markers
note 6500 A marker is a place with a type and a short note: a hazard, a camp, water, a meet-up. Robin marks a washed-out bridge and tells Sage.
wait 7000
caption Robin wakes the radio and opens the Atlas: A.
wait 1500
key SPACE
wait 1500
key A
expect 8000 [MAP] open
wait 4000
caption J lists the markers, nearest first. The last row makes a new one.
wait 1500
key J
expect 3000 [MARK] list
wait 3500
key ENTER
expect 3000 [MARK] new
wait 3000
note 6000 Row 1 is the type: Enter steps through hazard, person, vehicle, camp, water, meet-up, other. Hazard it is.
wait 6500
caption Row 2: from my position (or at the map cross). Row 3: 200 m. Row 4: 4 steps the direction N, NE, E.
wait 2500
key 4
wait 1200
key 4
wait 2500
caption 5: a short note. Type it, then Enter.
wait 1500
key 5
wait 1200
type BRIDGE OUT
wait 1200
key ENTER
wait 2500
caption 6 saves it.
wait 1500
key 6
expect 3000 [MARK] #1 hazard
wait 3500
caption 2: send it, to a contact or a group. Enter sends it to Sage.
wait 1500
key 2
wait 3000
key ENTER
expect 30000 [MARK] send #1
wait 4000
caption 1: show it on the map: a triangle, with a dashed line from you.
wait 1500
key 1
expect 3000 [MARK] show
wait 8000
note 6000 Alt+J on the map makes a marker right at the map cross. K shows all the Atlas keys.
wait 6500
caption ^ (Shift) closes the map. Later the radio sleeps again.
wait 1500
key BACK
wait 1500
key I
wait 1500
serial idle now
wait 12000
rec off
quit
