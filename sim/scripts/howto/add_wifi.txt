# How-to video: add a WiFi network. Device hv_w1aw in Typical mode, If-found idle screen with the
# ARRL / W1AW return address. Networks in range come from its sim.ini (make_videos.ps1).
expect 60000 [BOOT] ready
serial wiz skip
serial found 1 ARRL Headquarters
serial found 2 Station W1AW
serial found 3 225 Main Street
serial found 5 Newington
serial found 6 CT
serial found 7 06111
serial found 8 USA
serial found 9 Please return - 73!
serial idlescr 3
wait 1000
key M
key S
wait 800
key 4
wait 800
key 2
wait 800
key 0
wait 1000
key BACK
wait 600
key BACK
wait 600
key BACK
wait 800
serial opmode 1
wait 1000
key I
wait 1500
serial idle now
wait 2500
rec add_wifi
caption The radio sleeps and shows where to return it: ARRL Headquarters, station W1AW.
wait 3500
caption Any key wakes it, back on the Inbox. (Typical mode: WiFi settings are on.)
wait 1200
key SPACE
wait 2000
caption Settings: M, then S. Then 3 (WiFi): hold SYM, press R.
wait 1200
key M
wait 900
key S
wait 1500
key 3
wait 2000
caption 3 again: Scan networks. The routers in range are listed, strongest first.
wait 1200
key 3
expect 8000 Async scan
wait 3000
caption Pick ARRL-HQ by its number: 3.
wait 1500
key 3
wait 2000
caption Type its password: HIRAMMAXIM. MIC shows / hides it.
wait 1200
type HIRAMMAXIM
wait 800
caption Enter joins. The radio remembers it and joins again by itself.
wait 1000
key ENTER
expect 10000 [WIFI] connected
wait 3000
caption Connected. ^ (Shift) goes back; after a while the radio sleeps again.
wait 1200
key BACK
wait 900
key BACK
wait 1500
serial idle now
wait 3500
rec off
quit
