# How-to video: waypoints on the Atlas. Device hv_robin at the Alamo (sim.ini gps), map tiles
# on its SD card (howto_seed\atlas).
gps on
expect 60000 [BOOT] ready
serial wiz skip
# wake it (a start ends on the idle screen): the first key does nothing else
wait 1500
key SPACE
wait 800
serial wpt clear
wait 3000
key I
wait 1500
serial idle now
wait 2500
rec Atlas_waypoints
note 5500 A waypoint is a place you mark on the map, so you can find it again.
wait 6000
caption Any key wakes the radio. A opens the Atlas on your own position.
wait 1500
key SPACE
wait 1500
key A
wait 3000
caption T F V H move the map: bring the place to the middle of the screen. (Z zooms in, W zooms out.)
wait 2500
key H
wait 1200
key T
wait 2000
caption Alt + P adds a waypoint at the middle of the map. Type a name, then Enter.
wait 1500
key ALT+P
wait 1200
type WELL
wait 600
key ENTER
expect 5000 [WPT] W1 WELL
wait 2500
caption Move on and mark a second place the same way: CAMP.
wait 1500
key F
wait 1000
key F
wait 1000
key V
wait 1500
key ALT+P
wait 1200
type CAMP
wait 600
key ENTER
expect 5000 [WPT] W2 CAMP
wait 2500
caption P steps through your waypoints. The bottom line shows the name, and how far and which way it is from you.
wait 1500
key P
wait 1800
key P
wait 3000
key P
wait 3500
caption To remove the waypoint that is selected: Alt + SYM, twice.
wait 1500
key ALT+SYM
wait 900
key ALT+SYM
expect 5000 [WPT] removed
wait 3000
caption The whole list is in Settings: M, S, 2 (Time & Location), 9 (Waypoints).
wait 1500
key M
wait 1000
key S
wait 1200
key 2
wait 1500
key 9
wait 4500
caption ^ (Shift) goes back. Later the radio sleeps again.
wait 1500
key BACK
wait 800
key BACK
wait 800
key BACK
wait 1200
serial idle now
wait 3500
rec off
quit
