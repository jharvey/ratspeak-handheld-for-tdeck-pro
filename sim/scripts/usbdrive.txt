# USB drive mode (S.9.5 row 6, console "usbdrive"): the SD card becomes a drive on the PC.
# Refused on battery; on USB the drive screen shows, the firmware keeps off the card
# (sdget fails), and the PC's Eject (sim: "usbeject") restarts the unit back to normal.
#   run-script.bat scripts\usbdrive.txt usbdrive
expect 60000 [BOOT] ready
wait 1500
serial wiz skip
wait 500
usb off
wait 2500
serial usbdrive
expect 3000 [USB] drive mode not started
usb on
wait 2500
serial usbdrive
expect 3000 [USB] drive mode:
wait 1500
shot usb-01-drive.png
serial sdget /batlog2.csv
expect 3000 [SDGET] err no SD card
key Q
wait 800
shot usb-02-key-ignored.png
serial usbeject
wait 1000
shot usb-03-ejected.png
expect 5000 [USB] drive mode off: restarting
