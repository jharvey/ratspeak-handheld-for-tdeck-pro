# are gone): 300 outgoing "unconfirmed" messages, 2 hours old, in 2 conversations, on a 1 MB
# flash. They must be removable, so the upkeep makes room and every save succeeds. (Before
# 2026-11-23 only delivered / failed outgoing messages could go: the flash filled and every
# new message was refused.)
# Run: ratspeak-sim.exe --name ffu --flashkb 1024 --fast --script scripts\fullflash_unconfirmed.txt --quit (fresh data)

serial msgfill 300 2 200 0 6 7200

serial flash
