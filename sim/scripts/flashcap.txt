# Fast full-flash check on the normal 8 MB flash: "flashcap 100" makes removal start after
# about 100 messages, then 300 fake 50-character messages go through the normal save path.
# Run: run-script.bat scripts\flashcap.txt flashcap fresh
wait 9000
serial flashcap 100
expect 3000 [FLASH] message cap
serial msgfill 300 4 50
expect 120000 [FILL] saved 300, failed 0
serial flash
expect 3000 removed for space
serial rmconv
wait 2000
devshot flashcap_home.png
quit
