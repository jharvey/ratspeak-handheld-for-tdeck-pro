# Energy trend graph (S.5.9 > 1): left mA axis (0, half, rounded max), a tick under every
# bar, clock-time labels under the bars (rotated), "now" under the newest. Bars are made up
# with the bench command "energy fill <n>" (30 min periods). 24 bars, then 4.
# Run: run-script.bat scripts\energy_graph.txt egraph fresh
usb off
expect 60000 [BOOT] ready
serial wiz skip
wait 1500
serial energy period 1800
serial energy fill 24
expect 3000 [ENERGY] period 1800 s, 24 closed
key M
wait 600
key S
wait 600
key 5
wait 800
key 9
wait 800
key 1
wait 1500
shot egraph-01-24bars.png
key BACK
wait 600
serial energy fill 4
expect 3000 [ENERGY] period 1800 s, 4 closed
key 1
wait 1500
shot egraph-02-4bars.png
key BACK
wait 400
key BACK
wait 400
shot egraph-03-power.png
serial setcheck
expect 5000 [SETCHECK]
quit
