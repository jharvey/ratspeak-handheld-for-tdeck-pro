# SOS in Check-in mode: the radio is normally off between windows and messages wait for the
# next window; an SOS turns LoRa on and goes out now. Ending it goes back to Check-in.
usb off
expect 20000 [BOOT] ready
serial wiz skip
wait 1500
serial contact 0123456789abcdef0123456789abcdef HELPER
wait 800
key M
key S
key 5
key 7
key 1
expect 3000 [POWER] Check-in
key BACK
wait 500
key BACK
wait 500
key BACK
wait 800
serial sos now
expect 5000 lora on
expect 5000 [SOS] sent to 1 of 1
serial sos
expect 3000 [SOS] ACTIVE
serial sos off
expect 5000 [SOS] cancel to 1 of 1
expect 5000 [SOS] off (console)
expect 30000 [TX] sent "SOS cancelled
expect 10000 lora off
quit
